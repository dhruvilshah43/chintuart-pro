Acrylic Letters

Acrylic letters are a versatile and stylish signage solution suitable for virtually any setting. Cut from solid acrylic sheets in custom shapes and colours, these letters can be backlit with LEDs for a modern glowing effect, painted in brand colours, or finished with metallic films for a premium appearance. Chintuart produces acrylic letters with precision CNC cutting for sharp, clean edges.

✓ CNC precision-cut from solid acrylic
✓ Available in 5mm–50mm thickness
✓ UV-stable with fade-resistant colours
✓ Optional LED backlighting
✓ Metallic, mirror or glossy finishes
✓ Wall-mounted or stand-alone options
