About This Service

Hoardings

Hoardings are large-format outdoor advertising boards that deliver powerful brand messaging to high-traffic audiences. Chintuart fabricates and installs hoardings in various sizes from small roadside boards to large building-mounted displays. All our hoardings use high-quality vinyl printing on durable frames with optional LED or spotlight illumination.

✓ Large format digital printing
✓ Steel or MS frame construction
✓ Front-lit and backlit options
✓ Weatherproof vinyl printing
✓ Building-mounted or freestanding
✓ Flex and ACP panel finishes
