SS Letters With Backside Solid Acrylic Letters

This premium combination combines the metallic elegance of stainless steel letter faces with solid acrylic backs that allow light to glow from behind, creating a distinctive halo illumination effect. The result is a high-impact, luxury signage look that is both dramatic and sophisticated — ideal for premium brands, jewellers, boutiques and hospitality establishments.

✓ SS face + solid acrylic back construction
✓ Halo (back-lit) LED illumination
✓ Mirror polished SS or brushed finish
✓ Custom colours for acrylic diffuser
✓ Rust-proof and weatherproof
✓ Available with gold or rose-gold plating
