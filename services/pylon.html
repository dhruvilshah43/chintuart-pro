Pylon Signages

Pylon signages are tall, freestanding sign structures that provide maximum visibility from a distance, making them ideal for highways, commercial complexes, hospitals, malls, and petrol stations. Chintuart designs and installs custom pylon structures in single-faced and double-faced configurations with full LED illumination for 24/7 brand visibility.

✓ Custom height from 6ft to 30ft+
✓ Single and double-faced options
✓ Full LED internal illumination
✓ Powder-coated steel structure
✓ Foundation-mounted for stability
✓ Branding on all visible sides
