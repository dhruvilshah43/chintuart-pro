Aluminum Channel Bending Letters

Aluminum channel bending letters are three-dimensional, professionally crafted letters formed by bending aluminum into precise shapes. Available with front-lit, back-lit (halo) or side-lit LED illumination, these letters deliver a premium, dimensional look that stands out on any facade. Ideal for hotels, malls, corporate offices, showrooms and large retail stores.

✓ Precision aluminum bending machine
✓ Front-lit, back-lit or halo options
✓ Powder-coated or raw aluminium finish
✓ LED-illuminated for night visibility
✓ Custom fonts and logo shapes
✓ Polished, matte or gloss finishes
