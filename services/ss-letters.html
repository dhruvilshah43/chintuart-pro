SS / Rose Gold / Golden Letters

Stainless steel, rose gold, and golden letters represent the pinnacle of premium signage. These letters are laser-cut and polished from high-grade stainless steel, then plated or painted in gold or rose gold for a luxurious effect. Perfect for jewellery stores, high-end boutiques, luxury residences, premium hotels, and any brand that wants to convey elegance and class.

✓ Laser-cut from Grade 304 stainless steel
✓ Mirror-polished or brushed finishes
✓ Rose gold and 24K gold plating available
✓ LED halo (back-lit) illumination
✓ Rust and corrosion proof
✓ Suitable for indoor and outdoor use
